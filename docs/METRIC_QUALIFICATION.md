# Final Metric Qualification

Qualified national long-term metrics (six): Copernicus/ERA5 air temperature, IMS air temperature, Copernicus/ERA5 relative humidity, UTCI, MRT, and IMS rainfall total. Multiplicity families are annual=6, DJF=6, global=12.

`IMS_RH_LONGTERM_STATUS=NOT_QUALIFIED`: only Beer Sheva and Negba provide continuous 1950–2025 coverage, which is not nationally representative.

`RAIN_DAYS_LONGTERM_STATUS=NOT_QUALIFIED`: national spatial representation is insufficient, particularly in Central Negev and Arava/Eilat, and the denser-network approach failed regional validation.

Neither excluded metric appears in final national reference outputs, inference families, or figures.
