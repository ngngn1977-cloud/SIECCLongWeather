# Data rights and attribution

This file records the rights position for the data used and shipped by SIECCLongWeather.
The MIT licence applies to the package code only. Data keep the terms of their source.
This is a statement of the author's reading of the provider terms, not legal advice.

## Israel Meteorological Service (IMS)

- Terms of use: https://ims.gov.il/sites/default/files/inline-files/termsofuse_en.pdf
  (Redistributed with attribution by author decision (14 Oct 2026); IMS terms of use URL recorded 2026-09-01.)
- Observation API: https://ims.gov.il/en/ObservationDataAPI
- **Raw IMS station observations are not included.** They must be obtained from IMS
  under its terms (the API needs an `APIToken` and acceptance of those terms).
  Raw IMS station metadata bytes are likewise not included.
- The package **does include derived national annual and December-February aggregates**
  computed from IMS observations (air temperature, rainfall). They are released with
  attribution to the Israel Meteorological Service, in line with the author's reading of
  the IMS terms of use. **Anyone using these series must cite IMS.**

Required attribution:

> Contains data derived from the Israel Meteorological Service (IMS) station observations; (c) IMS.

## Copernicus Climate Change Service (ERA5 and ERA5-HEAT)

- ERA5 (Hersbach et al., 2020), DOI 10.24381/cds.f17050d7.
- ERA5-HEAT / derived UTCI historical (Di Napoli et al., 2021), DOI 10.24381/cds.553b7518, version v1.1.
- Licence: Licence to use Copernicus Products, https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/terms.
- The ERA5 and ERA5-HEAT derived national series are released under that licence.
  Large source fields are not included and are reacquired from the Climate Data Store.

Required attribution:

> Contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information [2026]. Neither the European Commission nor ECMWF is responsible for any use that may be made of the Copernicus information or data it contains.
